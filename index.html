Workflow

How it works

At a high level, a task moves through the same general path every time — the specific resource it lands on depends on what the task needs and what's available.

1

You submit a task

Through the web app, desktop client, mobile client, or API/CLI — one place to describe what you need done.

2

GemmaNode analyzes the task

The request is reviewed at a high level to understand its general nature — coding, research, generation, analysis, and so on.

3

Available resources are considered

GemmaNode looks at what's currently available across the resource pool — local hardware, cloud compute, AI providers, and agent frameworks.

4

Work is assigned to a suitable resource

Based on general factors like capability, availability, and cost. The exact selection logic is internal — this page describes what happens, not how it's computed.

5

Independent work can run concurrently

When resources allow, multiple agents or parts of a task can execute in parallel rather than strictly one at a time.

6

Results come back to you

Output is collected, consolidated, and returned through whichever client you started from.

Difficult or uncertain tasks can optionally be escalated for manual review by a stronger model rather than forced through an automated path that isn't working. This is a deliberate fallback, not the default route for routine work.

Cost & control

Built on free & user-controlled resources

GemmaNode is designed to make the most of free tiers, open-weight models, and your own hardware — not to promise unlimited free compute forever.

Free-tier aware

Designed to make good use of free and low-cost resources, while respecting each provider's own limits and policies.

BYOK

Bring your own keys where a provider requires one — nothing shared, nothing hardcoded.

No forced subscription

The core, self-hosted experience isn't metered by GemmaNode itself.

Your hardware counts

Local compute is a first-class resource, not an afterthought — useful even without a dedicated GPU.

Provider limits still apply

Free tiers, quotas, and pricing are set by each provider and can change at any time.

No hidden metering

You can see which resource handled a task — no silent switch to a paid tier without your setup allowing it.

Privacy & control

Local by default, external when you choose

What stays on your machine and what doesn't depends on which resource actually handles a task.

🔒

Local resources

When a task runs on your own hardware, your code and files can stay on your machine according to how you've configured GemmaNode.

🌐

External providers

When a task is sent to an external AI provider or cloud resource, the relevant data leaves your machine and is subject to that provider's own service and terms.

We don't promise privacy guarantees beyond what the actual configuration provides. If a task is routed externally, treat it the same way you would treat sending that data directly to that provider.

Project status

GemmaNode is under active development. This is an honest snapshot, not a roadmap promise.

  • Local orchestration on your own hardware
  • Coordination across multiple free/open AI models

Experimental
