Resource pool

GemmaNode works with a broad, extensible pool of resources. Availability, quotas, and pricing for any third-party resource are set by that provider and can change — nothing here is a permanent guarantee.

Escalation
Provider independence: resources can be added, removed, or swapped in the pool without changing how you interact with GemmaNode. No single vendor is a hard dependency.
Cost & control

Built on free & user-controlled resources

GemmaNode is designed to make the most of free tiers, open-weight models, and your own hardware — not to promise unlimited free compute forever.

Free-tier aware

Designed to make good use of free and low-cost resources, while respecting each provider's own limits and policies.

BYOK

Bring your own keys where a provider requires one — nothing shared, nothing hardcoded.

No forced subscription

The core, self-hosted experience isn't metered by GemmaNode itself.

Your hardware counts

Local compute is a first-class resource, not an afterthought — useful even without a dedicated GPU.

Provider limits still apply

Free tiers, quotas, and pricing are set by each provider and can change at any time.

No hidden metering

You can see which resource handled a task — no silent switch to a paid tier without your setup allowing it.

For developers

Built to evolve with the AI ecosystem

GemmaNode's provider and agent boundaries are designed to be swappable, not hardcoded.

New models

Additional models can be added to the pool as they become useful.

New AI providers

Providers beyond the current set can be integrated over time.

New compute resources

Additional local or cloud compute sources can be supported.

New agent frameworks

The agent layer isn't limited to what's currently listed.

Open source

Fully inspectable code, free to fork, audit, and extend.

No black boxes

What's public stays high-level by design — but nothing is hidden about how to contribute.
